Privacy Policy

Spooki Labs · Last updated September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Spooki Labs ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our content automation application (the "App"). The App is a personal-use tool that generates and publishes video content to social media accounts that we own and operate.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or process personal information from the public. It operates on accounts owned by Spooki Labs and does not offer login, registration, or data submission features to third-party users.

When the App interacts with third-party platform APIs (such as TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube), it accesses only the account credentials and content necessary to publish media to those accounts, as authorized directly by the account owner.

2. How We Use Information

Any account access tokens obtained through platform APIs are used solely to publish content to the connected account(s) and are not shared, sold, or used for any purpose beyond that function.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties. Access tokens are transmitted only to the relevant platform (e.g., TikTok, Meta) as required to publish content, in accordance with each platform's own API terms.

4. Data Retention

Access tokens and related credentials are retained only for as long as needed to operate the App and are stored locally in a private, self-hosted environment. They are not retained by any third party on our behalf.

5. Your Rights

As the App is operated for personal accounts owned by Spooki Labs, requests regarding data access or deletion can be directed to the contact below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
